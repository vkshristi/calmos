# CalmOS

CalmOS is a personal operating system for focus, flow, and wellbeing.

Instead of tracking more, CalmOS helps you **decide better**:

* When to work
* What to work on
* How to stay calm and productive

---

## 🧠 The Problem

Modern productivity systems optimize for **output**, not **sustainability**.

They assume:

> More hours = More productivity

But in reality:

* Cognitive performance is **state-dependent**
* Energy, stress, and sleep directly impact focus
* Pushing harder often leads to **burnout, not results**

This creates a contradiction:

> **Productivity and burnout cannot coexist long-term.**

---

## 🔬 Scientific Foundation

CalmOS is built on principles from:

* **Cognitive Science** → Focus depends on mental energy and context switching
* **Behavioral Psychology** → Small daily patterns compound into performance
* **Human Performance Research** → Recovery is as important as effort

Key ideas:

* High stress reduces cognitive bandwidth
* Poor sleep degrades decision-making and attention
* Frequent context switching creates “flow leaks”
* Sustained performance requires **balance, not intensity**

---

## 💡 Core Insight

Most tools track behavior.

CalmOS asks:

> **What should you do today, given your current state?**

It learns from:

* Wellness signals (sleep, stress, mood)
* Focus behavior (sessions, duration, flow quality)

And converts them into:

* A **Flow Score**
* Actionable **insights**
* Daily **adjustments**

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

```text
Wellness + Focus → Flow Score → Insights → Better Decisions
```

CalmOS:

* Predicts your daily capacity for deep work
* Identifies patterns affecting your performance
* Suggests small changes to avoid burnout
